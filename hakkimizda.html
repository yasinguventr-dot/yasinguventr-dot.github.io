About (English)

Seç Kartını is an independent Turkish-language horoscope and tarot channel based in Turkey. Every day it publishes 12 short videos, one for each zodiac sign, on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. This website offers a zodiac guide, tarot card meanings, an explanation of our method, an FAQ, and our privacy policy and terms. Contact: seckartinitr@gmail.com
